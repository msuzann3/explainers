The one-line case

Across 22 individual-contributor AI Enablement roles — specialists, leads, architects, and hands-on program roles, no directors — the midpoint is ~$163K (median ~$161K). These are peers to the exact role Slipstream is hiring for. Independent market data agrees: Glassdoor puts AI Enablement at ~$139K, and comparable postings like the WSGR AI Enablement Specialist run $150–200K. A $70–80K offer is roughly half the IC midpoint and lands at or below the 25th percentile of even the broadest entry-inclusive data.

The Seattle cost-of-living wall

Seattle runs ~43% above the U.S. national average. A $70–80K salary doesn't just lag the market — it doesn't cover the basics here.

The take-home math

At $75K in WA (no state income tax), take-home lands around ~$5,000–5,100/month after federal tax and FICA. A Seattle rent of $5,500/month absorbs the entire net paycheck — before food, transport, utilities, or savings. National "comfortable" figures of $87K assume much lower rent; against real Seattle housing, the break-even floor is well above $135K.

Sources: Wise, Salary.com, LivingCost.org, Wealthvieu · 2026. Take-home estimate: single filer, WA (no state income tax), 2025–26 federal brackets + 7.65% FICA.
