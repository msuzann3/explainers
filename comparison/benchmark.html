The one-line case

The midpoint across 21 tracked salaried AI Enablement roles is ~$170K, and even the narrowest slice — roles literally titled "AI Enablement" — averages ~$142K. Independent market data agrees: Glassdoor puts AI Enablement at ~$139K and Director-level at $160–200K+. A $70–80K offer is roughly half the market midpoint and lands at or below the 25th percentile of even the broadest entry-inclusive data.

The Seattle cost-of-living wall

Seattle runs ~43% above the U.S. national average. A $70–80K salary doesn't just lag the market — it doesn't cover the basics here.

The take-home math

At $75K in WA (no state income tax), take-home lands around ~$5,000–5,100/month after federal tax and FICA. A Seattle rent of $5,500/month absorbs the entire net paycheck — before food, gas, utilities, or savings. National "comfortable" figures of $87K assume much lower rent; against real Seattle housing, the break-even floor is well above $120K.
